Media — the reference library

A private library
is just hoarding.

Everything I return to, in public. Not a canon and not a personality — a working set of references I actively steal from, with the reason I keep them attached.

Film

Mostly for how they handle light, restraint and the passage of time. Almost never for plot.

←→ Drag or use arrow keys

In the Mood for Love

Wong Kar-wai, 2000 — restraint as the most erotic available choice. Nothing happens, gorgeously.

Chungking Express

1994 — how fast you can build a world when you trust the audience to keep up.

Beau Travail

Denis, 1999 — narrative carried entirely by bodies and heat, with almost no dialogue.

Portrait of a Lady on Fire

Sciamma, 2019 — the argument that looking at someone carefully is the whole of intimacy.

Paris, Texas

Wenders, 1984 — colour as emotional infrastructure. That red sweater does real work.

Photography

The warm-toned, obstructed, slightly-too-close school — the register most of my art direction lives in.

Saul Leiter

Obstruction is composition. Half the frame is a rain-blurred window and the picture is better for it.

Nan Goldin

The case that access is the entire aesthetic. You cannot light your way to this.

Rinko Kawauchi

Overexposure as tenderness. Proof that a technically "wrong" image can be the honest one.

Luigi Ghirri

Ordinary Italian buildings, faded palette, total conviction. My reference for warm minimalism.

Reading

Essays I re-read

The ones that changed how I argue, not just what I think.

Ways of SeeingJohn Berger, 1972 — still the most useful thing ever written about advertising. Against InterpretationSusan Sontag, 1966 — the case against explaining a thing instead of experiencing it. Status as a ServiceEugene Wei, 2019 — the only social-media theory that has actually held up. The White AlbumJoan Didion, 1979 — how to write about a culture while admitting you're inside it. Thinking, Fast and SlowKahneman — the behavioural spine under most of how I read funnels. PLACEHOLDERAdd your own — this list should be unmistakably yours.
