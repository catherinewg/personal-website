Philosophy

Three
pillars.

Everything I do rests on these. They're the reason briefs get argued with before they get executed.

The longer version→

01

Become customer obsessed

Not customer-informed, not customer-adjacent. The customer's actual behaviour — what they clicked, what they abandoned, what they said when nobody was selling to them — outranks internal consensus every time.

02

Fail fast and learn

Shipping is how you find out. I'd rather run the campaign, read the data and be wrong in three weeks than be theoretically correct in a deck for six months.

03

Practice radical candor

Care personally, challenge directly. The most expensive thing in a marketing team is the objection nobody raised — and warmth is what makes the hard conversation survivable.
