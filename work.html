Jan 2025 — Marketing Lead Dripos

Sole marketer at a YC-backed vertical SaaS company raising its Series B

I joined Dripos to build and lead the marketing function. As the only marketer, I partnered directly with the co-founders to execute strategic initiatives, and collaborated with Sales to develop content and media that enhanced demos and drove inbound leads.

Being a team of one clarifies things. There's no handoff between strategy and execution, so the brief and the finished artefact answer to the same person — which is the closest I've come to the intersection I actually want to work at.

Project 02

Strategy
& analytics

We had no real method of collecting data on what kind of leads we were attracting, why, or how. Over six months I built the measurement layer the strategy had been missing.

I implemented Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Semrush and a UTM convention across channels — then, more importantly, built a retroactive review system so we were reading the analytics on a cadence and iterating strategy against them.

Instrumentation without a review habit is just a dashboard nobody opens. The habit was the deliverable.

Google Analytics Brief

8 minute read · 2025

Problem. Our marketing strategy lacked a clear, data-driven foundation. We consistently pushed content across multiple channels without the visibility to understand who our prospects were, how they engaged with the brand, or which efforts drove conversions — so we relied on guesswork rather than strategic decision-making.

Solution. Implementing Google Analytics moved us from a reactive strategy to a proactive, data-driven one, collecting and analysing data across touchpoints to give a clearer picture of the funnel.

Marketing Strategy Proposal

Strategy · 2025

Dripos had been steadily growing on Instagram and Facebook — case studies, feature highlights, customer spotlights, weekly launches — but engagement stayed low on Facebook and X, and the marketing lacked a systematic approach to conversions.

With Meta Pixel and Google Analytics in place, we could track audience demographics, user behaviour and campaign effectiveness, shifting from gut-feel marketing toward precise, high-impact strategy.
